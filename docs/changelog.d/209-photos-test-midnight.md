- **A test stopped failing for the first three hours of every day** (SUPERVISOR-12, found while diagnosing two red pull requests at 00:1xZ). `ContactsPhotosToolsTests` asked the photos tool for pictures taken **today** and required at least one back, while its fixture creates pictures taken **one, two and three hours ago**. Those are the same day for twenty-one hours and different days for three, so between 00:00 and 03:00 in the runner's timezone the tool correctly returned nothing and the suite called it a failure. Nothing was ever wrong with the product: the same commit passed at 23:47Z and failed at 00:08Z. The test now asks for the day its own fixture used, which is the behaviour it meant to check — filtering by a given day returns that day's pictures. Proven by ageing a fixture past midnight on purpose: asking for "today" finds nothing (the old shape fails) and asking for the fixture's day finds them.
